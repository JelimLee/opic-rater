# Question list (example)

One line per question, in the order they were asked. Any of `-`, `*`, or `1.`
works as the bullet. Everything else in the file is ignored, so keep your notes.

## Self introduction
1. Let's start the interview. Tell me about yourself.

## Combo 1 — beaches (background survey)
2. Tell me about your favourite beach. Where is it, what does it look like, and why do you like it?
3. What do you usually do when you go to the beach? Describe your routine from arriving until you leave.
4. Tell me about a memorable experience you had at the beach. When was it, who were you with, what happened?

## Combo 2 — household chores (unexpected topic)
5. What chores do you usually do at home? Tell me about them in detail.
6. What were your childhood experiences with chores like? How did you feel about them?
7. Have you ever faced a chore you could not finish? What got in the way?

## Role play
8. A friend invited you to a holiday gathering. Call them and ask three or four questions about the plan.
9. Something has gone wrong on your way there. Call your friend, explain, and offer two or three alternatives.
10. Have you ever had a plan cancelled or changed unexpectedly? What happened and how did you handle it?

## Advanced
11. Compare two people in your life. How are they alike, how do they differ?
12. What do you and the people around you usually talk about these days?
